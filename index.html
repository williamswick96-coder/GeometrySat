Area of Lunes on a 45°-45°-90° Triangle

The figure below contains a \(45^\circ\text{-}45^\circ\text{-}90^\circ\) triangle with hypotenuse length \(4\). Three semicircles have been constructed, with their centers at the midpoint of the hypotenuse and the midpoint of each leg. The shaded crescents in the figure are called lunes.

Figure: \(45^\circ\text{-}45^\circ\text{-}90^\circ\) triangle inscribed in the large semicircle of diameter \(4\)

Key Inline Formulas Reference

45°-45°-90° Triangle Hypotenuse \(h = s\sqrt{2}\)
Leg \(s = \frac{h}{\sqrt{2}}\)
Area of Triangle & Semicircle \(A_{\triangle} = \frac{1}{2}bh = \frac{1}{2}s^2\)
\(A_{\text{semi}} = \frac{1}{2}\pi r^2\)
Area Addition / Subtraction \(A_{\text{lunes}} = A_{\triangle} + A_{\text{small semis}} - A_{\text{large semi}}\)
